What Causes Water Damage in Atlanta Homes

  • Burst pipes in winter — Atlanta's occasional hard freezes catch uninsulated pipes off guard, especially in crawl spaces and attics.
  • Severe thunderstorms — spring and summer storms bring flash flooding, roof leaks, and fallen-tree damage.
  • Water heater failures — the average water heater lasts 8–12 years; when Atlanta units fail, they can release 40+ gallons indoors.
  • Crawl space moisture — common in Atlanta homes, leading to wood rot and mold under the house.
  • Appliance leaks — washing machines, dishwashers, and refrigerator ice makers are frequent culprits.

Basements & Crawl Spaces Need Special Attention

Many Atlanta-area homes have basements or crawl spaces where water collects unnoticed. Because Georgia's clay soil drains slowly, water can press against foundations for days after heavy rain. Professionals use moisture meters and thermal imaging to find hidden dampness behind walls and under floors — damage you'd never spot until mold or rot appears.
